## What this report is

This report matches markers in your file against published research and shows what
scientists have found associated with them. It is not a diagnosis and not medical
advice.

## How to read this report

- The evidence tier (robust, speculative, or limited) reflects how much research
  supports each association — how many studies, how large, how consistent.
- Associations come from studying groups of people. A marker linked to a trait in a
  study says something about that group, not a prediction about you.
- Epigenetic age depends on sample type. A clock built for blood gives a meaningful
  number only on blood; where it does not apply, this report says so instead of
  showing a figure.

## Privacy

Your file is processed on this server and shared with no one.

## Talk to a professional

Talk with a doctor about anything here that concerns your health — they can weigh it
against everything else they know about you.
